MoMA PS1 Selling Points

Price

The PS1 is free, which means I can visit it again and again in short succession. In the coming year it will even be free for out-of-town visitors !

Currency

The PS1 is Manhattan's premier contemporary art museum, so the art is newer, the artists are often alive, and the relationship between me, the art, and the artist feels a little more two-sided and conversational.

You are also able to find a higher concentration of techniques and media at the PS1 that were partly or wholly unknown to a lot of artists exhibited at the MoMA proper - digital art, audiovisual work, even some (tasteful) generative AI inclusions.

Location

I live right past 1st ave so it's a fun few miles to walk the Queensboro to Long Island City. I use it as an excuse to walk around the surrounding area and search for new vantage points of Manhattan or get a peek at some of those industrial garages with the doors open.

Obscurity

Idk why, but nobody goes to the PS1. It's never crowded, and selfishly I kinda like it that way.

Exhibitions

At least until March, PS1 has some heavy hitters in its lineup. Ayoung Kim's short film Delivery Dancer Codex is the crown jewel at the moment, and it's complemented nicely with the outlandish electric contraptions dreamed up in Four Dilations.
